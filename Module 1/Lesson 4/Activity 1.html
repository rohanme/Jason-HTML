Body fitness

Body fitness is a state of health and well-being that is achieved through regular physical activity, proper nutrition, and adequate rest. It involves maintaining a healthy weight, building strength and endurance, and improving cardiovascular health. Regular exercise can help prevent chronic diseases such as obesity, diabetes, and heart disease, while also boosting mental health and overall quality of life.

To be fit and active it is advised to do atleast 30 minutes of excise a day!

Tips to be healthy!

  • Eat a balanced diet
  • Stay hydrated
  • Get enough sleep
  • Manage stress
  • Avoid smoking and excessive alcohol consumption
