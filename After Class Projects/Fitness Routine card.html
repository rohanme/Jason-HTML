My Relaxing Night Routine

This page helps me track my winding down steps before sleeping. It uses a dark theme to rest the eyes.

Unplug Devices

Turn off the TV, put away your smartphone, and stop looking at glowing screens.

Time: 8:30 PM

Warm Herbal Tea

Brew a fresh cup of chamomile or peppermint tea to soothe your stomach.

Time: 8:45 PM

Read a Book

Read a few pages of a hardcopy fiction book to slowly relax your racing brain.

Time: 9:00 PM

Write Diary

Jot down three small things you felt thankful for today to end on a happy thought.

Time: 9:30 PM

Dim the Lights

Switch off main overhead lights and turn on a small bedside lamp or fairy lights.

Time: 9:45 PM

Deep Breathing

Lie down flat and take ten slow, deep breaths to completely calm your body down.

Time: 10:00 PM

Sleep Tip: Going to sleep at the exact same hour every night keeps your internal clock perfectly balanced.
