The Ultimate Nature Guide!

Let's learn all about the cool plants, tall mountains, fast rivers, and wild creatures outside.

High Mountains

Mountains are super tall rocks that touch the clouds. Lots of cool birds and goats live way up high on them!

Amazing Animals

Wild creatures live out in the open forests and deserts. We need to respect them and give them plenty of space.

Deep Woods

Green forests are packed with massive trees that clean up our air. Walking through them is very relaxing.

Running Water

Freshwater rivers flow down into giant oceans. They provide a drink of clean water to every living creature nearby.

My Camper Checklist

  • Pack comfortable walking shoes
  • Always throw trash in bins
  • Bring cameras for photos
  • Tell someone where you are going
