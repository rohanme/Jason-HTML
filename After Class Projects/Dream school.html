My Morning School Routine

A step-by-step guide to my morning preparation:

  1. Wake up at 6:30 AM and pack my school binder.
  2. Eat breakfast and grab my lunchbox.
  3. Brush teeth and put on my sneakers.
  4. Catch the school bus at 7:15 AM.
  5. Arrive at school and meet my friends at the lockers.
